Project 03

Hydraulic Arm

Within this project, the goal was to design a robotic arm that was capable of moving an object remotely. The arm would be hydraulically powered by syringes and be able to not only grab objects but also rotate.

Process

A temporary design was first tested using our hands as the hydraulics. This was important as we needed to determine where to place each syringe.

Since the syringes are only capable of pulling or pushing, we needed to evaluate each joint separately with its own hydraulics.

Next, wooden frames were cut out using a bandsaw to get the target shape. Since the arm would need to move, the wooden frames would have to not only be connected, but also have joints that allow for rotation. We decided that the best approach to this would be to drill a hole into the frame and place a dowel into the hole to allow for movement.

Once the body was made and the syringes were connected, the arm was capable of moving. However, the hydraulics were not strong enough to move the arm fully. To solve this issue, we went back to the workshop to cut off extra weight to make the body lighter. Following this redesign, the arm was now capable of moving.

Outcome

The final design was not only capable of moving the target object, but was the smallest and most cost-effective design.
