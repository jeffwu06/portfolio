Project 02

Walking Robot

The goal of this project was to design a linkage mechanism connected to a gearbox capable of free movement.

Major skills

CATIA v5 - CAD & FEA Simulations MATLAB - Displacement, Velocity, & Linkage Simulations Manufacturing: Soldering, 3D Printing, & Laser Cutting Documentation & Research

Project Gallery

Process

To start, numerous designs were investigated. Since there were no restrictions on the number of legs, we decided to choose between two, four, and six-legged based movements. Since the criteria for the robot was for it to be free standing and as light as possible, we decided that the four legged design would be the best approach.

A first design was drawn and recreated within CATIA v5 to test the linkage movement. Afterwards, MATLAB was used to create the displacement, velocity, and linkage simulations to help optimize the linkage lengths and joint positions.

Lastly, manufacturing involved printing out the individual CAD components from CATIA and connecting the gearbox to the printed components. A combination of laser cut hardboard and 3D printed components were used for the design. Lastly, a battery was connected to the motorbox via soldering.

Through testing, we found a few errors with our design. One of them was that the torque was not strong enough to rotate the linkages. We fixed this issue by adjusting the gear ratios to allow for more torque generated by the motor. Another issue found was the robot not walking straight. The reason for this was that the legs were not aligned properly, and the centre of mass was not in the middle of the robot.

After these changes, the robot was finally capable of moving.

Outcome

The final design placed 5th in the overall competition of around 32 teams. This project helped me reinforce numerous skills such as CAD, MATLAB, and designing linkages. Not only that, manufacturing skills such as 3D printing, laser cutting, and soldering.
