Project 04

Flight Simulator

Developed a flight dynamics simulator in MATLAB’s Simulink to simulate real-time flight data, including 6-DOF orientation, velocity, and real-time latitude, longitude, and altitude.

Add a hero image or video here

Overview

Implemented a physical throttle and joystick to control the plane’s ailerons, elevators, rudders, and engine thrust in real time.

Process & results

Created a realistic visual environment of the simulator by linking Simulink to FlightGear, creating an integrated 3D interface system.
