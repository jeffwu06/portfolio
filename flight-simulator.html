Project 04

Flight Simulator

The goal of this project was to design a flight physics simulator for a Boeing 737. This involved calculating the plane’s 6-dof, velocity, and GPS-coordinates. Physical controls such as a joystick and throttle were also implemented along with the software FlightGear to allow for a visual interactable interface.

Major skills

MATLAB - Simulink FlightGear Documentation & Research

Project Gallery

Process

To calculate the 6-dof, the individual formulas for the position and orientation vectors were isolated from their respective matrices.

A map diagram of each formula and their respective variables were identified, then implemented in MATLAB’s Simulink.

Furthermore, the plane’s aerodynamic dimensions and characteristics (mass, span, chord length, etc.) were all found via official sources.

Outcome

The final product was able to successfully replicate the plane’s physics during flight. To confirm the validity of our results, a graph was generated for various values such as Velocity vs. Time, Altitude vs. time, and compared to the correct results from the assignment document.

This project taught me how to use MATLAB’s Simulink and the process of calculating aerodynamic variables.
